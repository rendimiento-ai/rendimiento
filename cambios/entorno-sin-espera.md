- **Environment:** the live settings no longer wait for the cluster. Which apps are outside the wall is now read on its own (the nodes, and the apps' pods only when a node does not enforce network policies), kept 30 seconds, and never the whole services catalog, which listed every pod, workload and service in the cluster on each look. When the cluster is slow to answer (during an update, for instance) the settings come at once and that one line shows a placeholder until the answer arrives, a few seconds later.
