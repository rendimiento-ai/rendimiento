- **Speed:** Visitors (`GET /api/stats/visits`, an app's Visits tab) no longer wait on Umami each time: its websites and each site's numbers are kept 2 minutes, an older answer (up to an hour) is shown at once while a new one is read behind it, a failed read keeps the old one, and the sites are read in parallel (6 at once). The first read waits at most 10 seconds; past that the page says Umami is slow and to try again in a moment, while the read goes on. "Today" no longer misses the cache every minute.
- **Speed:** The Environment page's report (`GET /api/environment`) is still new for 15 seconds, but an older one (up to 10 minutes) now answers at once while the next is made behind it, instead of every look waiting for the checks; the cluster's lists (nodes, pods, namespaces, deployments, services, metrics, certificates) are read at once, not one after another; a report takes 30 seconds at most. `?refresh=1` still waits for a new one, and a DNS sync still makes the next look wait for one.
- **Speed:** The platform's version card (`GET /api/platform/version`) keeps its answer 30 seconds (an older one, up to 10 minutes, answers while the next is read), so a look no longer waits for the database, the cluster and GitHub's compare; Update forgets it.
- **Speed:** The delivery panel (`GET /api/stats/delivery`) runs its three queries at once and keeps the report a minute (an older one, up to 30 minutes, answers while the next is read).
