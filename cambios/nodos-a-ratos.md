- **Nodes «a ratos»:** a computer that joins the cluster only sometimes (taint and label `rendimiento.ai/a-ratos=true`) helps CI while it is on: builds also make the image's other architecture there and join both halves in one OCI index, tests whose images have that architecture may run there, and a step cut short by its shutdown runs again once on another node. While it is off, nothing waits for it, and Environment draws it asleep instead of down. Needs the `buildkit` add-on's `buildkitd-a-ratos` daemon.
