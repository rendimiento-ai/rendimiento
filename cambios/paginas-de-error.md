- **Errors:** a page that cannot be shown says what happened, why it may have, and what to do: *This page does not exist* (an unknown address, or an app, run or request that is not there; it suggests an app with a close name and says where you came from), *You have no access to this page*, *Something failed showing this page* (the interface failed; the top bar stays, with *Try again* and *Tell the team*), and *rendimiento does not answer* (it retries every 5 seconds by itself).
- **Problems:** each of those pages shown is recorded once on the Problems page, grouped by kind and page, with where the person came from and the version, never who saw it (`POST /api/fallas`).
