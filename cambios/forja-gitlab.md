- **GitLab:** apps can live on GitLab too, gitlab.com or your own. Connect one on **Environment → Where your code lives** with an OAuth application of yours (scopes `api` and `read_user`); **New app** then lists its projects in a GitLab tab, and rendimiento adds each project's webhook, clones with the OAuth token, writes a commit status on every commit and opens and merges merge requests («merge when pipeline succeeds» when GitLab's pipeline still runs). See the book's «Connecting GitLab».
- **Sign-in:** the welcome page signs in with any connected GitLab too. Link your GitLab account from **Your accounts** (the account's menu) to be one person in rendimiento; an unlinked account comes in as `username@host` when it is a member.
