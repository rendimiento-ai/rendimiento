- **Visits:** opening an app's page no longer waits for Umami. Above its tabs and on its overview it asks only for the totals and the little chart (two of Umami's queries instead of a whole report's eight), rendimiento reads those ahead for every app when it starts and every half hour, and kept numbers stay up to an hour without anyone looking. Before, each app's page asked for two whole reports, and the first look after an update or a quiet half hour waited several seconds: `GET /api/apps/{app}/visits` was the API's slowest route (109 answers over a second in a week, 4 s on average).
