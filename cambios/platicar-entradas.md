- **La Ruta:** every entry but lo vivido opens in two tabs, «The entry» and «Talk it over · N»: a conversation about the entry, kept with it, that anyone who sees the entry can join, every message signed (a person, the AI with its model, or an agent's key). Each person chooses who answers from the AIs the AI page offers now, with what a message costs (the platform's, your own key, the local model for free), or «My agent». The AI reads the entry, the related entries and the book's matching pages, says what it read, only talks, and keeps «The idea so far», which people may correct.
- **Encargos from a conversation:** «Done: make encargos» has the AI propose them, priced by the estimator; each is kept or left out, with its title, app (required), difficulty, who does it and what it comes after, and «+ Add another». «Make them» or «Make and send» (to the coding agent); «Save it as a decision in the Ruta» writes a decision entry linked to the one talked about. Each encargo carries the conversation, the idea and the decision.
- **AI page:** «Talking entries over» sets what one conversation may spend on paid models before it asks whether to go on (50 cents by default).
- **Agents (MCP):** `platicas_esperando` and `platica_responder`: a person's own agent answers the conversations that chose «My agent», signed with its key's name, in a cargo.
- **Database:** migration `0064_platicas` only adds tables (`platicas`, `platica_mensajes`) and a column with a default (`ai_settings.platica_cap_micros`); the previous version reads none of them.
