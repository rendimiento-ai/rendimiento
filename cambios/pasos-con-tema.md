- **Runs:** a run's steps no longer say «side by side» on every step: they show as **the race** (each step a runner in its lane across the run's time, with the place it arrived in and its duration, a dotted stretch before the ones that waited) or as **the threads** (the run as a weaving: a thread from the change for every step that waits for nobody, as long as the step took, and a step that waits for others starts at the knot of their threads). A switch on the steps chooses, and the browser remembers it; both grow live while the run goes.
