- **Language:** a first visit picks Spanish or English by the first of the browser's languages that is one of them (in their order of preference); a browser that asks for neither (say, only Portuguese) starts in its country's, from Cloudflare's `CF-IPCountry` header: Spanish for Spanish-speaking countries, English for the rest. The language chosen with the switch still wins, and a request without `Accept-Language` (a crawler) is answered in English as before. The page gets only "es" or "en" (`<meta name="rendimiento-lang-pais">`); the country is never kept or logged. Without Cloudflare nothing changes.
